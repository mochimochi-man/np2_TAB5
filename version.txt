np2 TAB5


1.0.0   2026-09-03   first release

  PC-9801 emulator for the M5Stack Tab5 (ESP32-P4). Forked from np2 espresso,
  the ESP32-S3 emulator, which is itself based on NP2kai.

  - MIPI-DSI panel driven directly rather than through the BSP, which picks its
    panel by board revision and gets this one wrong. The panel is identified at
    boot from the touch controller: ST7121, ST7123 or ILI9881C. Only the ST7121
    has been tested on real hardware.
  - Rotation and scaling on the PPA, the P4's 2D pixel engine.
  - A compatible BIOS and font are built into the firmware, so the emulator
    starts from a blank SD card with no ROM dump present.
  - USB keyboard and mouse on the USB-A port; Bluetooth LE keyboard and mouse
    through the companion ESP32-C6; the touch panel acts as the PC-98 mouse.
  - SD Card Reader Mode and Disk Image Reader Mode over USB - the latter
    presents the DOS volume inside the mounted hard disk image to the PC.
  - GreaseWeazle Mode: reads a real floppy through a GreaseWeazle on the USB-A
    port and writes it to the SD card as a .NFD.
  - Screenshots written to the SD card as PNG.
